Sound Familiar?

You've poured your heart into your manuscript. You've edited until your eyes blurred, rewritten entire chapters at 2 a.m., and finally typed "The End" with tears streaming down your face. Your book is brilliant. You know it. Your beta readers know it. Your mum definitely knows it.

But here's the brutal truth that nobody in the publishing industry wants to tell you out loud: a great book does not sell itself. Not anymore. Not in a marketplace where Amazon adds thousands of new titles every single day and readers are overwhelmed with more choices than at any point in human history.

Right now, over 4 million new books are published every year across all formats and platforms. Without a strategic, multi-channel marketing plan, your book, your beautiful, brilliant, years-in-the-making book, will be buried on page 47 of a search result that nobody ever scrolls to. Your cover will go unseen. Your blurb will go unread. Your story will go untold.

That is not a failure on your part. It is a failure of visibility. And that is exactly where I come in.

I don't just market books. I make sure the right readers find your book, fall in love with your book, and tell everyone they know about your book. From the first 3D mockup that catches a reader's eye on Instagram to the Goodreads Listopia placement that keeps your book visible for years, I build campaigns that work at every stage of the reader journey.

The Person Behind the Campaigns

I'm Orla Penroseby, and I'm Obsessed with Getting Books into Readers' Hands

Over the past decade, I've built a career around a single, unwavering belief: every great book deserves to be read. Not just by a handful of loyal friends and family members who will buy it out of politeness, but by the thousands, sometimes hundreds of thousands, of readers out there who are actively searching for exactly the kind of story or knowledge you've put into the world.

I started in this industry over ten years ago when I joined the publicity department at a well-known London publishing house. It was there, surrounded by towering stacks of advance copies and frantic launch-day spreadsheets, that I learned the hard truth about the publishing industry: the quality of a book has almost nothing to do with its commercial success. That experience struck me as deeply unfair, and more importantly, deeply fixable.

Since going independent, I've had the privilege of working with over 150 authors across every genre imaginable. Literary fiction and sweeping historical romances. Gritty thrillers and fast-paced sci-fi. Self-help guides and children's picture books. The scale changes, but the strategy, the care, and the commitment never do.

Real Results from Real Authors

How a Debut Author with Zero Platform Hit Number One on Amazon in 5 Days

15,000+ Launch Week Downloads
#1 Amazon Category
400% Ad Spend ROI
150+ Reviews in 30 Days

When S.M. first emailed me, she was eight months away from publishing her debut literary fiction novel and she was, in her own words, "terrified." She had no social media following, no email list, no industry connections, and a marketing budget that was, frankly, modest. But she had a genuinely extraordinary book, and she was willing to put in the work.

I designed a 14-week pre-launch campaign that began with a complete Amazon listing overhaul, rewriting her book description, identifying 12 high-traffic, low-competition keywords, and selecting niche categories where she could realistically compete. We then launched a targeted ARC campaign, distributing 200 advance copies to carefully vetted reviewers. I also produced a cinematic book trailer that generated over 10,000 views in the first week alone, and secured placement on 5 high-traffic Goodreads Listopia lists.

The result? Her book hit number one in its primary Amazon category within five days of publication, sold over 15,000 copies in its first week, and generated a 400% return on ad spend. Six months later, it's still selling 500+ copies per month organically.
