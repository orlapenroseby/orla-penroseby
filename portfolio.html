Literary Fiction Author: S.M. | 16-week campaign | Budget: £2,500

The Debut Literary Fiction Launch

#1 Amazon Bestseller (UK)
15,000+ First-Week Sales
400% Ad Spend ROI
150+ Reviews in 30 Days
3,500 New Instagram Followers
2,200 New Email Subscribers
12,000+ Book Trailer Views
5 Listopia Top-10 Placements

The Challenge

S.M. first emailed me eight months before her publication date with a beautifully written debut novel and a marketing budget that was, in her words, "embarrassingly small." She had no social media presence, no email list, no industry connections, and no prior publishing experience. She was also, understandably, terrified that her book would disappear into the void, a fear that's entirely justified given that over 100,000 new literary fiction titles are published every year.

The Strategy

I knew that with a limited budget, we couldn't outspend the competition, so we had to outsmart them. I started with a deep competitive analysis of the 20 most successful literary fiction debuts from the past 12 months, identifying the specific marketing tactics, timing, and channels that drove their success. Based on this research, I built a 16-week campaign that focused on three high-impact pillars:

  1. Amazon dominance: I completely overhauled her Amazon listing, identifying 7 niche subcategories where she could realistically compete for bestseller status (instead of the 2 overly broad categories she'd originally selected). I rewrote her book description from scratch, transforming it from a generic blurb into a compelling, emotionally resonant sales page. I also optimized all 7 backend keyword slots with long-tail phrases that her target readers were actively searching for.
  2. Review velocity and visual impact: I coordinated an ARC campaign distributing 200 advance copies to carefully vetted reviewers across NetGalley, BookSirens, and my personal network. I also produced a cinematic book trailer and a set of 5 custom 3D mockups that were used across all social media content and ad creatives, dramatically increasing engagement rates. The goal was to have 50+ reviews live on Amazon by launch day, a critical threshold for Amazon's algorithm to start recommending the book to new readers.
  3. Community-driven buzz: I built her Instagram presence from zero, focusing on the Bookstagram literary fiction community. Through strategic engagement, micro-influencer partnerships (8 creators with 5K to 20K followers), a coordinated BookTok push in the final two weeks, and strategic Goodreads Listopia placements on 5 high-traffic lists, we created genuine, organic excitement that no amount of paid advertising could replicate.

The Results

The book hit number one in its primary Amazon category within 5 days of publication and remained in the top 10 for three consecutive weeks. First-week sales exceeded 15,000 copies. The Amazon Ads campaign, which ran on a modest £15/day budget, generated a 400% return on spend. The book trailer accumulated over 12,000 views across YouTube and Instagram. Six months after launch, the book continues to sell 500+ copies per month organically, and the Goodreads Listopia placements continue to drive steady traffic.

"Orla turned my terrifying launch into the best week of my writing career. Every single detail was handled via clear, organized emails, and the results were beyond anything I imagined possible." S.M., Literary Fiction Author
Thriller / Crime Author: J.O. | 8-week campaign | Budget: £1,800

The Backlist Revival

3x Sales Increase
8,000+ New Email Subscribers
25+ Media Features
4.8% ACOS (vs 35% avg)
3 Book Club Picks

The Challenge

J.O. is a mid-career thriller author with three published books and a fourth on the way. When he first emailed me, his monthly sales had flatlined at around 200 copies across his entire backlist, his email list was stagnant at 1,200 subscribers, and he was seriously considering giving up on writing altogether. His publisher had stopped investing in his marketing after his second book underperformed, and he had no idea how to revive his career on his own.

The Strategy

I started with a complete audit of all three books' Amazon listings and discovered that his metadata was catastrophically outdated: wrong categories, generic keywords, and book descriptions that hadn't been updated since his first publication. I overhauled all three listings simultaneously, created new 3D mockups for each book, and produced a short book trailer for the series as a whole. I then ran a backlist price promotion (Book 1 at £0.99 for one week) backed by targeted Amazon Ads and a BookBub Featured Deal application. Simultaneously, I launched a Book Club Litopia outreach campaign targeting thriller and crime book clubs, which resulted in 3 confirmed picks within the first month.

The Results

Monthly sales across the entire backlist tripled within 60 days, from 200 to over 600 copies per month. The email list grew from 1,200 to over 9,000 subscribers through a combination of the price promotion, reader magnet funnel, and Book Club Litopia outreach. The Amazon Ads campaign achieved an ACOS of just 4.8%, compared to the industry average of 35 to 40%. J.O. has since signed a new two-book deal with his publisher, who cited his improved sales trajectory as the primary reason.

"I was ready to quit writing before I found Orla. My backlist was dead, my email list was flat, and I had no idea what to do next. Within two months of her taking over, my sales had tripled, my inbox was full of readers who actually wanted to hear from me, and three book clubs had picked my series. She saved my writing career, and she did the entire thing through clear, organized emails that I could read and respond to on my own schedule." J.O., Thriller Author
Contemporary Romance Author: P.S. | 10-week campaign | Budget: £1,200

The Romance BookTok Breakthrough

200% Visibility Increase
50,000+ BookTok Views
#3 Amazon Romance
4,000+ New Subscribers
8 Listopia Top-20
15,000+ Trailer Views

The Challenge

P.S. is a self-published romance author who had released two books to modest sales and was struggling to break through in one of the most competitive genres on Amazon. When she emailed me, her books were ranking on page 12 for her primary keywords, her Amazon metadata was essentially invisible, she had no social media presence to speak of, and her monthly sales had dropped to under 50 copies. She had tried running Amazon Ads on her own but had burned through £400 with almost nothing to show for it.

The Strategy

I started with a complete Amazon listing overhaul for both books, rewriting the descriptions from scratch, identifying 14 new high-traffic keywords, and moving both books into niche subcategories where they could realistically compete. I then created a set of 5 custom 3D mockups and a 60-second book trailer specifically designed for BookTok and Instagram Reels, using warm, dreamy visuals and trending audio that resonated with the romance reader community.

The centrepiece of the campaign was a coordinated BookTok push. I identified 10 romance micro-influencers (creators with 5K to 25K followers who had high engagement rates) and provided them with free copies, the book trailer, the 3D mockups, and suggested talking points. I also launched a Goodreads Listopia campaign, adding both books to 25 relevant romance lists and running a vote generation strategy that pushed one of her books into the top 20 on the "Best Contemporary Romances of 2026" list within three weeks.

The Results

Organic Amazon visibility increased by 200% within the first month. A single BookTok video created by one of the micro-influencers went semi-viral with over 50,000 views, driving a massive surge in sales that pushed the book to number 3 in its Amazon Contemporary Romance subcategory. The book trailer accumulated over 15,000 views across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. The reader magnet funnel I built generated over 4,000 new email subscribers in 10 weeks. Six months later, P.S. is consistently selling 300+ copies per month across her backlist, and her Goodreads Listopia placements continue to drive organic traffic every single day.

"I spent two years trying to figure out book marketing on my own and got nowhere. Orla achieved more in 10 weeks than I had in two years. The BookTok strategy alone changed everything, and the Goodreads Listopia placements keep driving sales even now, months after the campaign ended. Best investment I've ever made." P.S., Romance Author
Self-Help / Mindfulness Author: Dr. E.V. | 12-week campaign | Budget: £3,500

The Non-Fiction Thought Leader

10,000+ Copies Sold (90 Days)
12 Podcast Interviews
500% Email List Growth
2 National Media Features
6 Book Club Picks
20,000+ Trailer Views

The Challenge

Dr. E.V. is a clinical psychologist who had written her first book on mindfulness and anxiety management. She had exceptional credentials and a genuinely life-changing book, but she had absolutely no author platform, no marketing experience, and no idea how to reach readers beyond her existing therapy clients. When she emailed me, she was four months from publication and her publisher (a small independent press) had allocated a marketing budget of just £300. She knew the book could help thousands of people but had no idea how to get it in front of them.

The Strategy

I positioned Dr. E.V. as a thought leader rather than just a first-time author. The entire campaign was built around her expertise and credibility. I started by producing a professional book trailer that featured statistics about anxiety combined with calming visuals. I also created a set of 10 custom 3D mockups that were used across all marketing materials, giving the book a polished, authoritative look that matched its serious subject matter.

The core of the campaign was a podcast pitch offensive. I identified 50 podcasts in the wellness, mental health, and self-improvement space and sent personalized pitches highlighting Dr. E.V.'s clinical credentials and the unique angle of her book. Simultaneously, I launched a Book Club Litopia outreach campaign targeting wellness-focused book clubs, corporate reading groups, and university psychology departments, providing each with a custom discussion guide and a written Q&A document. I also secured two features in national publications through targeted press outreach and managed a BookBub Featured Deal that generated over 3,000 downloads in a single day.

The Results

The book sold over 10,000 copies in its first 90 days, far exceeding the publisher's 12-month projection. Dr. E.V. appeared on 12 podcasts with a combined audience of over 500,000 listeners. The Book Club Litopia campaign resulted in 6 confirmed book club picks, including two corporate wellness programs that ordered 100+ copies each. Her email list grew from 200 to over 3,000 engaged subscribers. The book trailer accumulated over 20,000 views across YouTube and LinkedIn. Most importantly, Dr. E.V. has since been approached by a major publisher for a second book deal, and she credits the visibility we built during this campaign as the catalyst.

"As a first-time author with zero marketing knowledge, I was completely overwhelmed. Orla made the entire process feel manageable and even exciting. She explained every step in detailed emails, delivered on every single promise, and the results exceeded my wildest expectations. The podcast interviews alone have transformed my career. I now have a waiting list for my therapy practice and a second book deal. None of this would have happened without Orla." Dr. E.V., Self-Help Author
