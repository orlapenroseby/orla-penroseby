The Story Behind the Strategy

I Believe Every Great Book Deserves to Be Read, and I've Built My Career on Making That Happen

My name is Orla Penroseby, and I am, at my core, a reader. Before I was a marketer, before I was a strategist, before I ever looked at a click-through rate or an Amazon sales rank, I was the kid who read under the covers with a torch long after bedtime. I was the teenager who spent her lunch breaks in the school library. I was the university student who chose her modules based on which reading list looked the most exciting. Books have shaped my entire life, and the idea that a brilliant book could go unread simply because nobody knew it existed genuinely keeps me up at night.

My professional journey into book marketing began over ten years ago when I joined the publicity department at a well-known London publishing house. It was there, surrounded by towering stacks of advance copies and frantic launch-day spreadsheets, that I learned the hard truth about the publishing industry: the quality of a book has almost nothing to do with its commercial success. I watched extraordinary debut novels disappear without a trace because they were given a £500 marketing budget and a prayer, while mediocre celebrity memoirs soared to the top of the charts on the back of massive campaigns. It struck me as deeply unfair, and more importantly, deeply fixable.

After several years in traditional publishing, I realized that the authors who needed me most weren't the ones at the big houses with dedicated marketing teams. They were the self-published authors pouring their life savings into their dream. They were the indie press writers with brilliant books and tiny budgets. They were the traditionally published midlist authors whose publishers had given up on them after a disappointing first quarter. So I left the corporate world and founded my own book marketing consultancy with a clear mission: to give every author, regardless of budget or publishing path, access to the kind of strategic, professional marketing that was previously reserved for bestsellers.

Today, I work with a carefully curated roster of authors across the UK, US, Canada, Australia, and beyond. I've marketed over 150 books across 12 genres, managed campaigns with budgets ranging from £669 to £50,000, and helped more than 50 authors achieve bestseller status on Amazon, the Sunday Times, and the USA Today list. My services have expanded to cover every element of modern book marketing, from book trailers and 3D mockups to Goodreads Listopia campaigns and Book Club Litopia outreach. But the metric I'm most proud of isn't sales figures or rankings. It's the fact that 98% of my clients come back to work with me on their next book. In this industry, that kind of loyalty is rare, and I don't take it for granted.

Five Things That Set Me Apart from Other Book Marketers

I'm a Reader First, a Marketer Second

I read over 80 books a year across genres: literary fiction, romance, thriller, fantasy, memoir, self-help, children's, you name it. This isn't just a hobby; it's a professional advantage. Because I'm deeply embedded in reader communities, I understand what makes readers tick, what language resonates with them, what tropes they love, and what makes them click "Add to Cart" versus "Maybe Later." I don't market to demographics. I market to readers.

I Build Strategies, Not Checklists

Too many book marketers sell you a generic "launch checklist" (post on Instagram three times a week, run a giveaway, send a press release) and call it a strategy. That's not a strategy. That's a to-do list. A real strategy starts with deep research into your specific book, your specific audience, your specific competitive landscape, and your specific goals. Every campaign I build is bespoke, and I can explain the why behind every single tactic in clear, written detail.

I Think in Years, Not Weeks

Most book marketers focus obsessively on launch week and then vanish. I think differently. Launch week is important, yes, but the real money in publishing is in the long tail. I design campaigns that build sustainable, compounding visibility over months and years. That means evergreen SEO, backlist promotion strategies, email list growth, Goodreads Listopia placements that keep driving traffic for years, Book Club Litopia outreach that generates ongoing word-of-mouth, and reader community building that continues to generate sales long after the initial buzz has faded.

I'm Obsessively Transparent, and Everything Is in Writing

I've heard too many horror stories from authors who hired marketers and had no idea what was actually being done with their money. That will never happen with me. All communication happens via Gmail, which means you always have a complete, searchable record of every strategy decision, every weekly report, every budget breakdown, and every result. You'll receive a detailed written report every Monday morning showing exactly what was accomplished, what the metrics look like, what we're testing next, and a full breakdown of ad spend. I treat your budget as if it were my own.

I'm Honest About What's Realistic

I will never promise you a number one bestseller if the data doesn't support it. I will never tell you that a £669 budget will achieve the same results as a £3,500 budget. I will never inflate my past results or use misleading metrics to impress you. My reputation is built on honesty, and I'd rather lose a client by being upfront about limitations than keep a client by making promises I can't deliver on.

When I'm Not Marketing Books...

You'll find me curled up in a corner of my local independent bookshop with a stack of new releases and a dangerously large cup of Earl Grey. When I'm not reading or working, I hike the coastal paths near my home with my dog, attempt ambitious baking projects that rarely go to plan, and maintain a fiercely competitive Scrabble habit.

The Principles That Guide Every Campaign I Run

Integrity Above All

I will always tell you the truth, even when it's not what you want to hear. If your book cover isn't genre-appropriate, I'll tell you in a clear, respectful email. If your blurb isn't converting, I'll explain exactly why and how to fix it. Trust is the foundation of every successful partnership, and I protect it fiercely.

Relentless Curiosity

The book marketing landscape changes faster than almost any other industry. TikTok trends shift weekly. Amazon's algorithm updates monthly. Reader behaviour evolves yearly. I invest heavily in my own ongoing education so that my clients always benefit from the most current strategies available.

Genuine Empathy

I understand how deeply personal your book is. It's not just a product to be marketed. It's a piece of your heart that you've spent years creating. I approach every project with the sensitivity and respect that deserves. I celebrate your wins as if they were my own, and I take your setbacks personally.

Measurable Impact

Beautiful campaigns are lovely, but they don't pay the bills. Every strategy I recommend, every tactic I implement, and every pound I spend on your behalf is tied to a specific, measurable outcome. If I can't measure it, I question whether it's worth doing. Every result is documented in your weekly email reports.
