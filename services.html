How I Can Help

Comprehensive Book Marketing Services Designed for Every Stage of Your Author Journey

The biggest mistake I see authors make is trying to do everything at once, or worse, doing nothing at all because the options feel overwhelming. Effective book marketing isn't about doing everything. It's about doing the right things, in the right order, for your specific book and audience.

Book Launch Campaigns

Your book only launches once. Let's make sure it counts.

Most authors treat their book launch like a single event. The reality is that a successful launch is a 16 to 20 week process with careful planning, precise timing, and coordinated activity across multiple channels.

What I do, in detail:

  • Market and competitive analysis for your genre and audience
  • Amazon listing and metadata optimization
  • ARC strategy, review building, and launch materials
  • Pre-order strategy and social content planning
  • Campaign execution across launch week and post-launch promotion

Timeline: 16 to 24 weeks total

Investment: From £1,500 for a standard launch

Social Media and Author Platform Building

Stop posting into the void. Start building a community of readers.

I create platform-specific content plans that help your book become visible to the right audience without spending hours on content that never converts.

  • Content strategy across BookTok, Bookstagram, Instagram, and Facebook
  • Brand voice and visual identity guidance
  • Monthly calendar creation and engagement support
  • Reader community growth and relationship building

Timeline: Minimum 3-month commitment

Investment: From £800/month

Amazon and Retailer Optimization

60% of book buyers start on Amazon. Is your book visible?

Amazon is where discovery happens. I optimize your listing so the right readers can find you and your book converts when they visit.

  • Keyword research and backend optimization
  • Category and metadata refinement
  • Description and A+ content rewrite
  • Author Central and retailer setup improvements

Timeline: 2 to 3 weeks

Investment: £669 one-time optimization

Paid Advertising Management

Every pound you spend on ads should earn you more than a pound back.

I manage campaigns for Amazon, Meta, BookBub, and Google so your ad spend is focused on the readers most likely to buy.

  • Campaign strategy and setup
  • Audience targeting and keyword research
  • Creative testing and optimization
  • ROI reporting and monthly refinement

Timeline: Ongoing, minimum 2-month commitment

Investment: From £669/month plus ad spend

Email Marketing and Reader Funnels

Social media algorithms change. Your email list is yours forever.

Your email list is an asset you own. I build reader funnels and newsletters that convert interest into sales while keeping you connected to your audience.

  • Email platform setup and strategy
  • Reader magnet and landing page design
  • Welcome sequence and launch emails
  • Promotional campaigns and list segmentation

Timeline: 3 to 4 weeks

Investment: £669 setup and £350/month ongoing support
